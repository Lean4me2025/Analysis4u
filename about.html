About

Meet the analyst behind Analysis4U

I’m Drew Harris — a process, data, and improvement specialist who believes people make better decisions when the numbers tell a clear, honest story.

For years I’ve worked with organizations, city programs, county organizations and business owners to develop processes, track incidents, understand and improve performance, and make real changes. One of my ,long-running projects reduced monthly incidents at a site from the high 60s down to around 26 — simply by tracking the data, seeing the patterns, and adjusting the approach.

My background includes Lean Six Sigma Black Belt training, along with experience in ITIL, DevOps, and project management. That means I’m comfortable in both technical and operational environments — from IT and systems work to frontline operations and security.

Many places don’t have dashboards, KPIs, or formal analytics. They just know “something isn’t right.” My role is to come alongside you, gather what you have, and translate it into insight you can act on.

I speak both languages: the language of data and the language of real-world operations. Analysis4U is where those two meet.

What you can expect from me:
  • Plain-language explanations – no jargon fog.
  • Honest analysis – not dressed-up numbers.
  • Actionable recommendations – not just charts.
  • A toolkit shaped by Lean Six Sigma, ITIL, DevOps, and project management experience.
  • Respect for your time and your reality on the ground.
