About Analysis4U

Analysis4U exists to help cities, businesses, and security organizations turn scattered reports and raw numbers into clear, usable insight you can act on.

Who’s Behind Analysis4U

My name is Drew Harris. I hold a Bachelor of Science in Industrial Engineering (B.S.I.E.) and I am a certified Lean Six Sigma Black Belt.

For years I’ve helped organizations:

  • Reduce incidents and risk exposure
  • Eliminate waste and inefficiency
  • Improve operational flow and performance
  • Transform messy or scattered data into clear, actionable insights

I’ve applied these disciplines across city services, security operations, business operations, analytics programs, and executive decision environments. Over and over, the same truth appears:

Most organizations already have the data — they just can’t see what it’s trying to say.

Analysis4U is how I bring engineering precision, operational discipline, and continuous improvement directly to you — without the corporate-consulting overhead or complexity.
